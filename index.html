What does P1.8 mean, and why does it matter?

expand_more

P1.8 means the centres of the pixels are 1.8mm apart. The smaller the pixel pitch, the sharper the image looks up close. Most hire walls are P2.5 or P3; a P1.8 wall stays crisp even when visitors are standing right in front of your stand.
